RESUME-AFTER-CLOSE — owner asked, not yet built
The owner asked whether saving on floor 24 could return them to floor 24.
Honest answer: not today. Every floor is generated with plain random
numbers that leave no memory behind, so a finished floor cannot be
rebuilt once it is gone. Two ways to build it, each a batch of its own:
(a) store the finished map itself in the save, or (b) give the generator
a "seed" — a starting number that makes the same floor come out again —
which means threading that through every random call in floor generation.
The owner likes the idea; it already sits on the STILL TO COME list.

DECISION REVISED 16 Oct (replaces the identical-floor idea recorded earlier):
when resume is built, the FLOOR IS REBUILT FRESH and the run REWINDS to how it
was when the player entered that floor — gold, items, XP, level, traits and
HEALTH, with full health on entry, so somebody who quit at 3 HP does not have to
re-clear the floor while nearly dead. Rewinding is what stops a resumed floor
being farmed for chests and kills; rebuilding fresh means the game never has to
store a map, which makes this far cheaper to build than the earlier idea. The
cost accepted: quitting before the killing blow is an escape from death, paid for
with that floor's loot and XP. The title screen's CONTINUE button already holds
the hook this needs, and it will read the floor number once the feature exists.
